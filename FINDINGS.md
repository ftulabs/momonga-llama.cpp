What survives is a validated traffic model - prefill time per token is proportional to `distinct_experts(ub)/ub`, and the +11% at 48 landed inside the corrected +10-16% band - which is the quantity that explains the 2.4x at `-ub 512` and the ~300 MB of expert weights moved per token at `-ub 32` | Closed: no free width |
